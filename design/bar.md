# The bar

References: discord.com hero (1440×900) and themekaverse.com world-map hero (1440×900).
Every line can be checked by looking at a screenshot.

1. **Headline letterforms are wide and heavy with square terminals.** The display face is a
   black-weight extended grotesk: counters are wide, stroke ends are cut flat, and there are no
   rounded terminals. The headline is set in 3 stacked all-caps lines, with line gaps so tight
   (line-height ≤ 0.9) that ascenders nearly touch the line above.

2. **Scale contrast:** headline cap height is at least 2.5× the body x-height, and the hero uses
   at most 3 text sizes (headline, body, small chrome). Body copy is a single size in a lighter
   neutral than the headline.

3. **The world is the brightest, most saturated thing on screen and fills at least half the
   first viewport.** The UI (nav, text, forms) is visibly quieter than the art: lower saturation,
   and it never competes for the eye.

4. **Blurple appears on exactly one element per screen: the primary CTA.** Every other colour is
   either part of the art or a semantic status (online, whisper, room). The CTA row pairs a
   filled primary with a quieter secondary, or stands alone.

5. **Depth comes from layering, not shadows.** At least one layer (props, particles, background
   glow) sits in front of or behind the main art at a different scale or softness, creating
   parallax depth. No element has a drop shadow.

6. **Title lines carry progressive emphasis (MekaVerse):** earlier lines are visibly dimmer,
   and the final line is full contrast. Each line sits on a rule that spans that line's width.

7. **Chrome whispers:** nav and meta labels use one small size (≤ 12px), mono or tracked
   uppercase, with at least 32px between nav items, in a flat strip with no background block,
   so the page reads as art first and UI second.
